WHAT THIS VEHICLE ACTUALLY IS
The Santa Cruz is a unibody "sport adventure vehicle" (Hyundai's term). It drives like the Hyundai Tucson SUV it shares a platform with, but with a 4-foot bed in back. It is NOT a body-on-frame truck. It will not off-road like a Tacoma, and it will not tow like an F-150. What it WILL do: haul bikes, kayaks, mulch, furniture, dogs, and weekend gear while driving like a normal crossover. If your son wants a real truck, this isn't it. If he wants a daily driver that can do light truck things without the truck headaches, this fits.

ENGINE NOTE
This specific vehicle has the base 2.5L engine (191 hp). Reviews say it's adequate for daily driving but feels strained with a full load or towing. The optional 2.5L turbo (281 hp) is what most reviewers recommend. Since this is the base engine, expect reasonable but not exciting acceleration.

BIG PICTURE WARNING
Multiple reports (including KBB) indicate Hyundai plans to discontinue the Santa Cruz after the 2026 model year in favor of a larger midsize pickup. This could affect long-term parts availability and resale value, though the shared Tucson platform mitigates some of that risk.
