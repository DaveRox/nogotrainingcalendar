TRAINING: What is a Center of Influence?
==================================================
Professionals who:
• Have access to people who need financial advice
• Already have the trust of their clients or contacts
• Can refer business without 'selling'

Common COIs for financial advisors:
→ Attorneys (estate planning, business)
→ Accountants and CPAs
→ Real estate agents
→ Business brokers
→ Other financial advisors
→ Doctors and medical professionals
→ Community leaders

==================================================
TRAINING: The 5-Step COI Framework
==================================================
Step 1: IDENTIFY
  Make a list of 20 potential COIs in your area

Step 2: RESEARCH
  Learn about their business before reaching out

Step 3: CONNECT
  Offer value first (not a pitch)

Step 4: NURTURE
  Build the relationship over 2-3 touchpoints

Step 5: ASK
  Then — and only then — ask for referrals

Success Metric: Maintain 10 active COI relationships

==================================================
TRAINING: Step 1: Identify Your COIs
==================================================
Where to find potential COIs:

• Local bar association — Estate planning attorneys
• CPA society — Tax accountants
• Real estate agent associations
• Business networking groups (Rotary, Lions, etc.)
• Chamber of Commerce
• LinkedIn searches by industry and location

Your Assignment This Week:
Write down 20 potential COIs with:
• Name
• Profession
• How you know them (or could be introduced)

==================================================
TRAINING: Step 2: Research Before Connecting
==================================================
Before you reach out, learn about them:

• Visit their website — What services do they offer?
• Check their LinkedIn — What content do they share?
• Read their bio — What's their background?
• Look for common ground — Same college? Same community?
• Find recent news — Did they win an award?

Why it matters:
"I read your article about..." stands out from
"Hey, wanna partner up?"

==================================================
TRAINING: Step 3: Connect with Value
==================================================
Never lead with a pitch. Lead with value:

Option A: Share useful information
"I saw your post about estate planning. I work with clients on similar issues and thought this article might be helpful for your clients."

Option B: Offer a free resource
"We put together a retirement planning guide that your clients might find useful. Happy to drop off a few copies."

Option C: Suggest a meeting
"I'm building relationships with professionals in the area. Would you be open to a 15-minute coffee chat?"

==================================================
TRAINING: Step 4: Nurture the Relationship
==================================================
Relationships aren't built in one meeting. Nurture them:

• Connect on LinkedIn — Engage with their posts
• Send relevant articles — "Saw this and thought of you"
• Invite to events — Webinars, lunch-and-learns
• Personal notes — Birthday, holiday cards
• Refer business to them — Before you ask for anything

Rule: Give 3 times before you ask once.
Make referrals to them without expecting anything in return.

==================================================
TRAINING: Step 5: Ask for Referrals
==================================================
When to ask:
• After a positive interaction
• When they've mentioned a client with a need
• After you've referred business to them

The exact words:
"I've really enjoyed getting to know you. I'm looking to help more [business owners / families / retirees] in the area. Do you know anyone who might benefit from a conversation about [specific need]?"

Get two names:
"Who else do you know who might find this helpful? I'd be happy to handle the introduction."

==================================================
TRAINING: Common COI Mistakes to Avoid
==================================================
Mistake #1: Asking too soon
  → Don't ask for referrals in the first meeting

Mistake #2: Being vague
  → Say exactly who you're looking for

Mistake #3: Not following up
  → Set a specific follow-up date

Mistake #4: Taking without giving
  → Refer to them first. Be a source, not just a taker.

Mistake #5: Giving up too fast
  → COI relationships take 3-6 months to mature
