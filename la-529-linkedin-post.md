# Louisiana Parents: You Need to Know About This

If you have kids in Louisiana and you're not using a 529 plan, you need to read this.

I'm not going to bore you with everything a 529 is. Here's what matters: Louisiana has its own state-sponsored college savings plan called START, and using it saves you money. Every dollar you contribute gets you a state income tax deduction. Not a credit — a deduction, which still means more money in your pocket come tax time.

But here's what most Louisiana parents don't realize: this isn't just for college anymore. The rules changed a few years back, and now you can use 529 funds for K-12 private school tuition too — up to $10,000 per year. So if you're already considering private school, this makes the math even better.

The way it works is simple. You pick how aggressive you want your investments to be, or you can set it and forget it with a target-date fund that automatically adjusts as your kid gets closer to college. The plan handles the rest.

Yes, there are caveats. This is an investment, so returns aren't guaranteed. And the tax benefit is a deduction, not a credit — it reduces your taxable income rather than giving you a dollar-for-dollar offset. You should talk to a financial advisor about how it fits your specific situation.

But the core point is simple: if you're already putting money away for your kids' education, why not get a tax break while you're doing it? Louisiana is giving you a benefit here. Taking it isn't clever — it's just ignoring free money.

The 529 also gets you tax-free withdrawals for qualified education expenses at the federal level. So you get the state deduction now and tax-free growth later. That's a pretty good combination.

If you're a Louisiana parent and you've been putting this off, don't. The math is on your side.
