# Thomas Aquinas: A Graduated Learning Playlist on Spotify

**For:** Dave Guilford
**Built:** July 29, 2026
**Approach:** Start accessible, escalate to the real thing. Catholic framing welcome. Authority and reliability are the screening criteria throughout.

---

## How This Is Organized

Three phases. Each one ramps up the difficulty. You can move through them at your own pace, but the order matters. Trying to read the Summa cold, without the groundwork, is how people quit on Aquinas.

Every source here is either (a) from the Dominican Order, the religious order Aquinas himself belonged to and the institutional custodians of his thought, (b) from a credentialed academic or institution, or (c) a primary text by Aquinas himself. I excluded the "someone reads Wikipedia into a mic" tier entirely.

Every title below is a direct clickable link to the show on Spotify. Click it, hit Follow or Save, and it lands in Your Library.

## PHASE 1: THE ON-RAMP

*Goal: Understand who Aquinas was, why he matters, and get a feel for his mind before you touch the Summa.*

### 1. Audiobook: Saint Thomas Aquinas by G.K. Chesterton

[**Saint Thomas Aquinas** by Gilbert Keith Chesterton](https://open.spotify.com/show/3O96J4NoY1oBxovwyyD5TQ)

The classic literary biography. Chesterton, a Catholic convert and one of the great prose stylists in English, wrote this in 1933. It's short, vivid, and gets you inside the man, not just the arguments. Etienne Gilson, one of the greatest Aquinas scholars of the 20th century, said of this book: "I consider it as being without possible comparison the best book ever written on St. Thomas." That endorsement carries more weight than any blurb on any other Aquinas book.

**Authority check:** Chesterton was not a theologian, but Gilson's endorsement is the gold standard. Read it for the portrait, not the technical philosophy.

### 2. Aquinas 101 - Course 1: Why Aquinas?

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 1: Why Aquinas?** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/4HosaSenEBrRG16QMkO5e2)

This is the front door. The Thomistic Institute is run by the Dominican Order (Province of St. Joseph), the same order Aquinas belonged to. They are the institutional custodians of Thomistic thought in the English-speaking world. This course is a structured, multi-episode introduction that explains why Aquinas matters and gives you the big picture before you dig into specifics.

**Why it's first after Chesterton:** It's produced by the people who actually train Thomists. You're learning from the tradition, not a commentator on the tradition.

### 3. Pints With Aquinas (selected early episodes)

[**Pints With Aquinas** by Matt Fradd](https://open.spotify.com/show/312eXMI31liKUHSx6U5p1H)

Matt Fradd is a popular Catholic apologist, not a scholar. But his show interviews the top Thomistic scholars alive today, and the early episodes in particular are accessible and conversational. Treat this as your "commute" content. You'll pick up vocabulary and context without feeling like you're in a lecture hall.

**Authority check:** Fradd himself is lower authority, but his guests (Dominican friars, philosophy professors, theologians) are the real deal. Listen for the interviews, not the banter.

### 4. Audiobook: Aquinas: An Audio Guide by Edward Feser

[**Aquinas: An Audio Guide** by Edward Feser](https://open.spotify.com/show/3g2StJKlDeuv1l0An44tQf)

Feser is a contemporary philosopher and one of the most respected defenders of Thomistic philosophy working today. He writes for an educated general audience, not specialists. This audio guide covers the Five Ways (the proofs of God), natural law, and Aquinas's metaphysics in a way that assumes you're smart but new to the subject.

**Authority check:** Feser is a credentialed philosopher (Pasadena City College) and author of the standard modern introductory text on Aquinas. This is a scholar speaking, not a podcaster summarizing.

## PHASE 2: INTERMEDIATE

*Goal: Start engaging with the actual ideas, structured and guided, but still mediated by a teacher.*

### 5. Aquinas 101 - Course 2: Introduction to Thomistic Philosophy

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 2: Introduction to Thomistic Philosophy** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/7BnMGOtvvduW6aLP7X7R4c)

Course 1 told you why Aquinas matters. Course 2 starts teaching you how he thinks. Metaphysics, epistemology, the structure of his argumentation. Still accessible, but you're now doing the work.

### 6. Aquinas 101 - Course 3: God and Creation

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 3: God and Creation** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/5J0U0BekTB50Ejfqiuwizn)

The Five Ways, the nature of God, creation ex nihilo, providence. This is where Aquinas's philosophical theology really starts, and the Thomistic Institute walks you through it carefully.

### 7. Audiobook: The Philosophy of Thomas Aquinas by Peter Kreeft

[**The Philosophy of Thomas Aquinas** by Peter Kreeft](https://open.spotify.com/show/1NKw3shglg4v3wurTQaCbm)

Peter Kreeft is probably the single most trusted popularizer of Aquinas in English. He's a professor of philosophy at Boston College, a Catholic convert, and the author of "Summa of the Summa" and "A Shorter Summa," the standard modern distillations of the Summa for students. This audiobook is him teaching Aquinas directly, in his own voice. If you only listen to one audiobook in this phase, make it this one.

**Authority check:** Kreeft is a credentialed academic with decades teaching Thomistic philosophy. He's the bridge between the scholarship and the interested layman.

### 8. Summa in a Year

[**Summa in a Year** by Austin Habash](https://open.spotify.com/show/3esPTruHHZAoZyzrDJ1Dv4)

A personal walk-through of the Summa, one article at a time, over the course of a year. Habash is not a scholar, he's a layman working through it himself and bringing you along. This sounds like a weakness, but it's actually useful here. He's a competent guide who's reading the actual text, not summarizing someone else's summary. Use it as a companion to the Aquinas 101 courses and Kreeft.

**Authority check:** Lower. This is a motivated layman, not a Dominican. But he's reading the primary text honestly and the episodes are short and digestible.

### 9. Godsplaining

[**Godsplaining** by Dominican Friars, Province of St. Joseph](https://open.spotify.com/show/0aIOz3chYeQZxsdjyJzlOb)

Dominican friars applying Thomistic thought to modern questions: culture, technology, politics, daily life. This is not a systematic course, it's a podcast that shows you how Thomism thinks in real time. Good for keeping momentum when you don't feel like a structured lecture.

**Authority check:** These are actual Dominican friars, trained in the Thomistic tradition. High authority for the tradition they represent.

### 10. The Catholic Theology Show

[**The Catholic Theology Show** by Ave Maria University](https://open.spotify.com/show/7naJja2ORp4st5RiqsbTZy)

Ave Maria University is a Catholic university with a serious theology faculty. This show covers Catholic theology broadly, but Aquinas is the backbone of Catholic theology, so he shows up constantly. Good for seeing Aquinas in the wider theological context.

## PHASE 3: THE DEEP DIVE

*Goal: Read the Summa itself, with the best guides available, and engage with Aquinas as a living theological authority.*

### 11. Aquinas 101 - Course 4: Principles of the Moral Life

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 4: Principles of the Moral Life** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/4xR7c3XPxLbimMuQTYhv0f)

Natural law, the structure of human action, ends and means. This is Aquinas's moral philosophy, which is the foundation of Catholic moral teaching.

### 12. Aquinas 101 - Course 5: Theological and Cardinal Virtues

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 5: Theological and Cardinal Virtues** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/3a8QSAgquSzjEAY5FYjAh6)

Faith, hope, charity (theological virtues) and prudence, justice, fortitude, temperance (cardinal virtues). The heart of Aquinas's ethics.

### 13. Aquinas 101 - Course 6: Christ and the Sacraments

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 6: Christ and the Sacraments** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/64WFOZ4E35hbdbVpbjlJ2A)

Christology and sacramental theology from a Thomistic perspective. This is where philosophy becomes theology proper.

### 14. Aquinas 101 - Course 7: Virtue

[**Aquinas 101 - Course 7: Virtue** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/2p8Opry3KQZctAXk5ZAaE0)

Deepens the virtue material from Course 5. If you've made it this far, you're doing real Thomistic moral theology.

### 15. Aquinas 101 - Science and Faith

[**Aquinas 101 - Science and Faith** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/0NZ5hac28CdBRhKdDtrpSv)

How Thomistic thought engages with modern science. Relevant if you're interested in the faith-and-reason questions, which Aquinas is central to.

### 16. The Thomistic Institute (main podcast)

[**The Thomistic Institute** by The Thomistic Institute](https://open.spotify.com/show/2pB2h3UfxVjPkAQwL3ckP5)

This is the Institute's general podcast: guest lectures from Dominican scholars, philosophers, and theologians. Not a structured course, but a deep well of high-authority content. Once you've done the Aquinas 101 curriculum, you can browse this for topics that interest you.

**Authority check:** Maximum. These are the scholars who train other Thomists. Dominican priests with doctorates in philosophy and theology.

### 17. Thomas Aquinas College Lectures & Talks

[**Thomas Aquinas College Lectures & Talks** by Thomas Aquinas College](https://open.spotify.com/show/3zRcjE27b2HEppiKi1Y7A1)

Thomas Aquinas College is a Catholic liberal arts college that teaches entirely from the Great Books, with the Summa as a central text. These are academic lectures and talks from their faculty and visiting scholars. More advanced and less produced than the Aquinas 101 courses, but very high quality.

**Authority check:** The college is a recognized Catholic institution whose entire curriculum is built around Aquinas. The lecturers are scholars who teach the primary texts.

### 18. Audiobook: Aquinas's Shorter Summa

[**Aquinas's Shorter Summa** by St. Thomas Aquinas](https://open.spotify.com/show/4ZcdxjSCZXfNKdLxR62oZ9)

This is Aquinas's own condensed version of the Summa (the Compendium of Theology). It's Aquinas himself writing in a more compressed form. If you've been through the Aquinas 101 courses and Kreeft, you're ready to start reading the primary text, and this is the accessible entry point.

### 19. Audiobook: Compendium of the Summa Theologica

[**Compendium of the Summa Theologica of St. Thomas Aquinas: Pars Prima** by Saint Thomas Aquinas, Bernardus Bonjoannes, Wilfrid Lescher](https://open.spotify.com/show/624GzuUp0KQfP3uq36fNOv)

A medieval compendium of the Summa created by Bernardo de Garza (Bonjoannes) and later edited by Fr. Wilfrid Lescher, a Dominican. This distills the full Summa into a more navigable form while staying faithful to Aquinas's own structure and language.

### 21. Audiobook: Ninety-nine Homilies of St. Thomas Aquinas upon the Epistles and Gospels

[**Ninety-nine Homilies of St. Thomas Aquinas upon the Epistles and Gospels** by Saint Thomas Aquinas](https://open.spotify.com/show/033gsW6eIaXB1x3N8CQvOc)

Aquinas's own preaching, not his systematic theology. These are homilies on the Sunday readings. If you've been immersed in the Summa, this shows you a different side of Aquinas: pastoral, biblical, devotional. It rounds out the picture from pure theology into the lived faith.

## BONUS: Summa Contra Gentiles

[**Summa Contra Gentiles** by St. Thomas Aquinas](https://open.spotify.com/show/5Zq65HucY9wltr1msKqQ6F)

Not in the main playlist because it's a separate major work, but worth knowing about. The Summa Contra Gentiles is Aquinas's other great summa, written partly as a manual for missionaries arguing with Muslim and Jewish thinkers. It's more philosophical and less explicitly theological than the Summa Theologica, and some scholars consider it the better entry point into Aquinas's natural theology. If you finish the main playlist and want more, start here.

## NOTES ON WHAT I EXCLUDED AND WHY

- **"St. Thomas Aquinas: A Beginner Guide of The Summa Theologica" by Edward Carbey** — The title has irregular Unicode characters suggesting a low-effort or AI-generated production. No verifiable authority. Excluded.
- **Catholic Answers Live, Catholic Stuff You Should Know, Catholic Inspiration, etc.** — General Catholic apologetics podcasts. Fine shows, but not Aquinas-focused. You'll get Aquinas incidentally through them, but they're not systematic. Skip unless you want background noise.
- **"St. Thomas Aquinas" by Donald Goergen** — A biography, but Goergen is a Dominican and this is a solid work. It didn't make the main list only because Chesterton's biography is stronger for the on-ramp phase and Kreeft covers the philosophical material better. If you want a second biography after Chesterton, this is the one to get.
- **"The Aquinas Catechism"** — Aquinas's catechetical instruction. Good content, but the Ninety-nine Homilies cover similar pastoral territory and are more directly Aquinas's own voice. If you want more after finishing the main list, search for this.
